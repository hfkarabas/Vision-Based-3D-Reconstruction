## Protocol and evidence levels

Search families: UAV photogrammetry/review; large-scale SfM; COLMAP/OpenMVS interfaces; PatchMatch MVS; geometric depth consistency/fusion; image/keyframe selection; prioritized/progressive MVS; uncertainty/quality-guided reconstruction; UAV NBV/path planning; multiresolution/resource-constrained reconstruction; surface extraction; photographic texturing; metric evaluation. Sources prioritized CVF/BMVA proceedings, authors' project pages, institutional repositories, journal publishers and upstream software repositories. Query phrases included exact titles of competitors and combinations of “partial reconstruction”, “image selection”, “computational budget”, “confidence”, “regional refinement” and “UAV”. Search-engine crawl/upload dates were not treated as publication dates.

Inclusion: method, benchmark or review directly informing geometry, computational allocation, asset quality or evaluation. Exclusion: unrelated multi-view clustering, medical image selection, patents returned incidentally, pure view-synthesis results without demonstrated geometric evaluation. Incidental irrelevant hits are not padded into the scholarly-record count. Conference/preprint duplicates are one record; stable author/title/year keys identify them. Venue/year uncertainty stays explicit. DOI is omitted where it was not verified; a stable primary URL is sufficient.

Review labels: **A** = abstract or author/publisher summary inspected; **M** = identified full-text methods/evaluation sections inspected, not replication or an assertion that every line was reviewed. All summaries below are paraphrases. Relevance/inclusion is a screening judgment, not evidence that a method is implementable locally.

Backward chaining from NBS (2016) identified Hornung (2008), Mauro (2014) and surface-based selection. Chaining through Mostegel (2018) identified matching-partner and quality-fulfillment comparators. Targeted later-work searches recovered ACMM, PatchmatchNet, APMRR-MVS and newer uncertainty work. This is limited forward discovery, not a complete cited-by graph. A formal export of citation links and broader screening remains required before a strong originality claim.

## Close-work methodological findings

L06 sections 2–3 aggregate density, triangulation-angle uncertainty and appearance/geometric saliency, then rank/select views. A new weighted quality score must be compared with this family rather than presented as unprecedented.

L07 section 2 formulates surface smoothness, camera-cluster size and covered-triangle constraints jointly; sections 3–4 alternate camera and surface assignments hierarchically. Regional world-space allocation and camera clustering therefore overlap directly with the proposal.

L08 sections 2.4–2.7 initialize SfM patches in an octree, expand into unoccupied cells and subdivide for detail; the later priority mechanism steers processing. A new scheduler around established software is operationally different from its internal patch engine, but prioritization itself is established.

L09 sections 3.1–3.4 propagate point covariance into an occupancy octree and balance unexplored space against uncertainty reduction, then optimize stereo arrangement. Section 4.1 uses an all-image PMVS reference for parameter exploration because public Middlebury ground truth is unavailable locally; section 4.2 separately reports benchmark evaluation. Do not conflate proxy agreement with independent ground truth. Using captured images rather than commanding a robot does not by itself remove the overlap.

L11 sections 3.2–3.3 select matching partners and greedily rank fixed captured-image clusters on a preliminary surface. Its fulfillment combines coverage, resolution, covariance-derived uncertainty and predicted success. This directly anticipates much of the proposed scoring. A feedback-driven measured-cost action policy might differ, but an absence claim requires deeper citation tracing and experiments, not intuition.

L18 propagation/refinement sections operate on within-image hypotheses. It is a technical neighbor in adaptive MVS, not evidence that the exact external task-scheduling problem has already been solved. Nor does that distinction prove our method novel.
