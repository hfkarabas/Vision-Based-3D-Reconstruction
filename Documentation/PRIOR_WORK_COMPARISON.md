# Prior work versus proposed research

Verdict: **mostly established** for the broad idea; **potentially differentiated but overlapping** for the narrower feedback/cost/evidence-dependence hypothesis. No demonstrated original contribution yet. Bibliographic details and review depth are in [the review](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). “Not established here” means the inspected evidence did not establish a feature; it is not a universal absence claim.

## What is demonstrated, and what is not

Additional 2026 overlap: [Dong and Li](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0031320326012148) explicitly calibrate grouped confidence under cross-view dependence; [Cilic et al.](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/13/4317) schedule reconstruction stages using measured computing/network costs. Only indexed publisher material was inspected here. The former is a calibration neighbor and the latter a distributed-placement neighbor; neither alone establishes or refutes a regional action-selection gap. Both must be part of the final novelty audit.

Demonstrated local limitation: historical native meshes exhibit gaps, disconnected components, crossing layers and incomplete photo texture. Independent full-scene COLMAP SfM now establishes much stronger registration capability on the available originals. That does not yet show which dense established-backend limitations remain.

Not demonstrated: a scheduler beats established internal view selection, the available photos support every missing surface, a textured mesh predicts valid geometric improvement, a quality score is calibrated, or a proposed contribution generalizes across scenes. Missing reconstructed area can be unobservable rather than computationally neglected. A policy must be permitted to abstain instead of fabricating terrain.

The strongest possible narrow distinction is **sequential, cost-calibrated selection among established reconstruction actions with dependency-aware support and precision safeguards**. The closest static ranking, partial-map NBV and progressive methods must be implemented or faithfully reproduced before calling this new. Texture support is a secondary asset-quality constraint; it must not reward a geometrically wrong textured sheet.
