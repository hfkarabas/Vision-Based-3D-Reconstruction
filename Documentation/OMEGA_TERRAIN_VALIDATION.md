# Terrain reliability and automatic building recovery

Research topic **Hesaplama Kaynakları Kısıtlı Büyük Ölçekli İHA Görüntülerinde Kalite Geri Beslemeli Bölgesel Adaptif 3B Rekonstrüksiyon**.

The earlier G0–G6 evidence and negative adaptive-reconstruction result were preserved. These runs assess existing photogrammetry, ground classification, automatic roof segmentation and LOD1 assembly. They do not measure the adaptive reconstruction hypothesis.

## Inputs, scope and data separation

The starting revision was `f68412887ab2f8b7018c9ce37c91a4a0397776c8`. The tagged 44-volume prototype and its subsequent 45-volume terrain repair were preserved. The latter is this experiment's Urban baseline. All new outputs live under `outputs/mission-omega/building-recovery/terrain-validation/` and are excluded from Git. `frozen_candidate.json` records the Urban material policy, 1 m terrain grid, 0.3 m RGB grid, fixed WHU checkpoint and pilot parameters before the new dataset runs. Acquisition and known-pose COLMAP adapters are recorded separately.

- **Urban** uses the AirZoo survey DOM/DSM for the product. The source TIFFs carry conflicting or missing CRS tags. The matching world files and survey XML establish the EPSG:4547 assignment and approximately 5.27 cm source cell. The product has a 1 m height grid, approximately 0.30 m photograph pixels and local origin `[401098, 3126798.5, 0]` metres. Its comparison with the cached UAV COLMAP surface is qualitative where vertical datum is unverified. The protected UAV-only result uses only reconstructed observations and does not use survey geometry. The UV atlas is not treated as an orthophoto.
- **Sheffield Cross** is pinned to Git revision `143f556ea3128835ab46cd694714f8f78319699b` (Unlicense). The archive SHA-256 is `5a56f8c1b8f651e7d274ab720e84252c084f8abcd70325e6c9fced70571ec22d`. It contains 172 JPEGs and the released GCP table. The first pilot used 36 original 4000 × 3000 JPEGs, `DJI_0054.JPG` through `DJI_0089.JPG`, from the overlapping flight strip, with 518 verified image pairs and 36/36 registrations. COLMAP used image features to estimate the cameras. Post-reconstruction similarity alignment uses camera EXIF positions projected in the survey's UTM zone 17N. The first alignment attempt used the wrong UTM zone and was discarded before rasterization. The corrected alignment had 0.279 m median camera-position residual relative to the EXIF positions used for alignment. The published GCP table has five positions at the same nominal height. It was inspected and excluded from fitting. An independent survey uncertainty and vertical datum were unavailable. [Dataset and license](https://github.com/pierotofy/drone_dataset_sheffield_cross/tree/143f556ea3128835ab46cd694714f8f78319699b)
- **JB3D** uses the `Flight_to_entrance` sequence of Zenodo record 22787920 v1 (CC BY 4.0). The archive MD5 is `0a53b51fd9d15273ff61f4d6e4aa2d13` and the ENU observer MD5 is `12d3cfe4cdbeb143d0bef4370777279a`. The adapter extracted frame indices 360–570 every six decoded frames. The selected 36 camera positions span 74.39 m with a median adjacent baseline of 2.59 m, and avoid the sequence's large heading transition. The supplied RADIAL camera intrinsics and per-frame poses were used. The authors' script applied the published ENU translation `[0.162605, -0.410978, 0.239793]` m. The official script ran with NumPy 1.25-style scalar formatting to keep its output valid COLMAP numeric text under NumPy 2. The Day 1 LiDAR is a withheld evaluation source. [Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/22787920) · [Pose correction](https://github.com/JB3D-Dataset/Multi-View-Stereo/tree/main/Translation_correction)

## Urban results and screenshot locations

The official WHU example reproduced 27 polygons. The 512 px/256 px overlap, batch-two run on Urban generated 93 polygons at probability threshold 0.5 and area cutoff 20 m² in 23.56 s. The local GeoAI 0.43.1 runtime used torch 2.10.0+cu128/CUDA 12.8 on the RTX 5070. The Apache-2.0 WHU EfficientNet-B4 + UNet++ checkpoint is `09df9efd323bbd3d56b98b4857129eb9b5baa2d3`, SHA-256 `922af7c96c0dc44256ab8b4d1a071f2151e0a921c997af80b55bb766bcc30dc6`. The alternate official GeoAI `BuildingFootprintExtractor` Mask R-CNN model ran with RGB weights, 512 px tiles, half-tile overlap and threshold 0.5. It generated 180 area-filtered candidates in 26.39 s. Its MIT checkpoint revision is `aa2b25d27b2c78f0e92c7b6a4813a571f9bc2288`, SHA-256 `3aea5d0da7803be31ff4e2e1e5ac747f15cab2a13d6bb1a9319b6a6ba8bc7bca`. TorchGeo 0.8.0 and its optional dependencies were installed in a separate ignored overlay. [WHU example](https://opengeoai.org/examples/building_detection_whu/) · [WHU checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/giswqs/whu-building-unetplusplus-efficientnet-b4) · [Mask R-CNN example](https://opengeoai.org/examples/building_footprints_usa/) · [Alternate checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/giswqs/geoai/tree/aa2b25d27b2c78f0e92c7b6a4813a571f9bc2288)

In the repeated Urban height audit, 31/93 candidates passed raster gates, 33 lacked positive roof relief, 21 failed ground consistency, seven were clipped and one lacked terrain support. The overlay shows partial low-roof outlines and false detections over parts of the court and vegetation. The review windows and pixel-to-polygon counts are in `urban/reports/low_roof_review.json`. The southern low-roof window intersects one WHU polygon. Its 101.86 m² mask component survived area filtering and the polygon failed the positive roof-relief gate. Two WHU polygons in the northwestern window failed ground stability and positive roof relief, respectively. The alternate model returned two proposals in that window. Its 180 scene candidates were kept as comparison evidence. No alternate candidate was added to the delivered model. No reviewed reference census was available to measure precision or recall.

## Pilot results and remaining limits

Sheffield's frozen pilot fused 391,735 points. The 1 m surface contains 25,088 supported cells out of 32,340. The clean terrain retained all 16,611 initial CSF ground cells and changed 19. Grid jumps above 1 m/m decreased from 31 to 30, and the maximum fell from 3.18 to 2.18 m/m. The observed 0.3 m RGB projection has 214,491 of 359,660 pixels. Its gaps and blended colors reveal the limits of projecting fused point colors. It is not a camera-orthorectified mosaic.

WHU detected four Sheffield polygons, with 0–3% accepted terrain support under the roof cores. The opt-in secondary trial inferred ground locally from surrounding classified UAV-only anchors at a 6 m maximum distance, adding 264 previously absent cells only where the reconstructed DSM has data. Two candidates passed the unchanged raster elevation gates. Blender rejected one because of roof clearance and one because of a foundation gap. Both terrain-only variants remain available. No Sheffield building precision, recall or absolute altitude is claimed.

JB3D's first pose-only COLMAP run produced 1,165,307 positive geometric depth pixels across 36 maps, but fused zero points. Supplying actual SIFT match tracks from a fixed-pose COLMAP triangulation gave 27,329 landmarks and 67,739 fused points, with maximum world-to-camera matrix change of 6.66e-16 and unchanged intrinsics. Dense stereo parameters and frame selection stayed fixed. The initial failed run remains in `jb3d-colmap/`, with the covisibility run in `jb3d-colmap-covisibility/`. The separate native automatic source-view trial kept the four-source budget and produced 85,349 fused points. Supported ground cells rose from 465 to 1,199, and terrain triangles rose from 604 to 1,586. The wider raster also lowered the fraction of observed RGB cells from 28.7% to 19.1%. Both terrain exports are visibly fragmented and yielded zero WHU building candidates. Keep both initial and tuned outputs for comparison.

The first Sheffield and JB3D covisibility COLMAP runs took approximately 178 s and 132 s, with per-process peak RSS near 860 MiB, measured at 0.2 s intervals. These figures exclude image download, frame decoding, GIS, inference and rendering. Urban did not rerun its valid cached reconstruction. Unity 6000.6.1f1/glTFast 6.21 could not import the Urban candidate because the Editor failed to connect to its local Package Manager. Blender compatibility was verified separately.

The preliminary JB3D LiDAR check used 99,596,746 published vertex records, matched byte-for-byte across two Zenodo download endpoints. It used the author's evaluation polygon, 0.3 m voxel sampling and no LiDAR registration or fitting. Occupied 1 m reference-cell coverage was 17.29% with temporal neighbors and 19.93% with automatic sources. On the same 3,126 cells, reconstruction-to-LiDAR median distance moved from 0.344 m to 0.349 m while P95 fell from 2.673 m to 2.456 m. LiDAR-to-reconstruction median fell from 0.591 m to 0.551 m. This prefix is a non-stratified sample. The first scope and the full-file check are stored separately.

## Reproduction

Run the PowerShell examples from the repository root. Use new output directories each time. Windows paths to the existing software are adjustable.

```powershell
$p = 'outputs/mission-omega/building-recovery'
$o = "$p/terrain-validation"
$g = 'outputs/airzoo-benchmark/geometric-root-cause-recovery'
$py = "$p/runtime/venv/Scripts/python.exe"
$cfg = 'configs/omega_terrain_validation_candidate.json'
$bl = 'C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.3/blender.exe'
$pdal = 'outputs/mission-omega/tools/gis/Library/bin/pdal.exe'
$u = 'outputs/mission-omega/terrain-cleanup/asset'

& $py scripts/omega_building_inference.py --rgb "$p/runtime/whu_sample.tif" --hub-cache "$p/runtime/hub" --output "$o/replay-whu-example"
& $py scripts/omega_building_inference.py --rgb "$p/inference_rgb.tif" --hub-cache "$p/runtime/hub" --output "$o/replay-whu-urban"
& $bl -b -t 4 --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/omega_terrain_review_blender.py -- --scene "$u/urban_terrain_cleaned_white_buildings.blend" --baseline "$u/urban_terrain_cleaned_white_buildings.glb" --policy $cfg --views configs/omega_terrain_validation_cases.json --section 400942 3126717 400976 3126717 --output "$o/replay-urban"

& $py scripts/omega_colmap_pilot.py --images datasets/omega-terrain-validation/sheffield/pilot-input --gps-crs EPSG:32617 --settings $cfg --colmap "$g/colmap-cuda/bin/colmap.exe" --pycolmap "$g/colmap-python" --output "$o/replay-sheffield-colmap"
& scripts/run_omega_pilot_surface.ps1 -Cloud "$o/replay-sheffield-colmap/dense/fused.ply" -Frame "$o/replay-sheffield-colmap/frame.json" -Python $py -Pdal $pdal -Blender $bl -HubCache "$p/runtime/hub" -Settings $cfg -Output "$o/replay-sheffield" -Name sheffield_terrain

$j = Get-Content "$o/jb3d-frames-v2/inputs.json" -Raw | ConvertFrom-Json
$depth = @($j.depth_range_m)
& $py scripts/omega_colmap_pilot.py --images "$o/jb3d-frames-v2/images" --fixed-model "$o/jb3d-frames-v2/fixed_model" --triangulate-fixed --stereo-pairs "$o/jb3d-frames-v2/stereo_pairs.txt" --depth-range @depth --settings $cfg --colmap "$g/colmap-cuda/bin/colmap.exe" --pycolmap "$g/colmap-python" --output "$o/replay-jb3d-colmap"
& scripts/run_omega_pilot_surface.ps1 -Cloud "$o/replay-jb3d-colmap/dense/fused.ply" -Projection $j.frame_crs_for_raster -Python $py -Pdal $pdal -Blender $bl -HubCache "$p/runtime/hub" -Settings $cfg -Output "$o/replay-jb3d" -Name jb3d_terrain
```

The image workflow requires physically overlapping input, a successful camera model and reconstruction, metric alignment, and sufficiently complete, correctly located ground and roof observations. The first trials used four source views, corroboration in at least three fused pixels, 640 px dense images, and a 2 GiB cache. The wrapper bounds grid dimensions and does not create a solid if the existing geometry checks reject it. For other scenes, first inspect acquisition overlap and coordinate conventions. Coarser or sparser color projections can confuse pretrained segmentation. Transfer performance from two pilots does not establish generalization.
